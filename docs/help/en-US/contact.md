# Contact Support

## Reporting an Issue

If you run into a bug, have a feature suggestion, or need help with credit billing, feel free to email us. When possible, please include:

1. The page and steps leading up to the issue;
2. A screenshot of any error message;
3. The approximate timestamp (to help us locate server logs).

## Contact Email

- **Email**: uniqueainn@gmail.com

We typically reply within 1–2 business days. For billing questions, please attach a screenshot from **Credit History** for faster review.

## Before You Reach Out

- Check the [FAQ](/help/faq) first — most questions about charges, failures, and limits are answered there.
- If a task failed, check **Credit History** to confirm whether credits were already automatically refunded.
