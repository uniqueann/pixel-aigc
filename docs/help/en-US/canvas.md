# Free Canvas Guide

Free Canvas brings reference images, generated assets, and editing workflows together inside one spatial project. Model availability reflects live platform status, and credit quotes appear before every submit.

## Text-to-Image

1. Select **Text to Image**, enter your prompt, choose model, resolution, aspect ratio, and count (target aspect pixels display right beneath).
2. Confirm the estimated credit cost and click generate — placeholders track elapsed time while generating.
3. Move, zoom, compare, or download finished images on the canvas.

## Canvas Workspace & Generation Aspect Ratio

The canvas is a fixed 1280×720 spatial board where batch outputs, variations, and pasted duplicates sit side by side. Changing **Aspect Ratio** in the right sidebar controls only the output dimensions of the **next generation**, not the canvas size itself.

Generated results appear centered in your current viewport so you can arrange, align, and duplicate them freely.

## Empty Canvas Prompts

A blank canvas offers three starter prompt chips (for both image and video modes): click any chip to prefill the prompt box and customize from there, or skip them via **Add Image** or drag-and-drop from your computer.

## Derived Generation & Continuing Edits

Use **Add Image** to upload local images or import assets from **My Assets**. Select any canvas image to branch into **Variations**, **Image to Video**, or **Image Studio** tools after confirming the credit quote.

## Video Generation

Text-to-Video, Image-to-Video, and Video Replication activate automatically when corresponding backend models are enabled. Choose model, duration, resolution, and audio options, confirm credit cost, and submit. Note video retention deadlines in My Assets.

MiniMax H3 supports 768p (50 / 100 credits for 5s / 10s) and 2K (80 / 160 credits for 5s / 10s) with native audio; Seedance supports toggling audio. Image-to-Video uses your selected canvas image as the first frame and matches its ratio. Check live buttons for active model pricing.

## Canvas Shortcuts & Layer Tools

- Scroll to pan, hold `⌘/Ctrl` + scroll to zoom, or hold `Space` + drag to pan; `⇧0` resets zoom to 100%, `⇧1` fits the board. Touchscreens support pinch-zoom and single-finger pan.
- Drag image files from your computer straight onto the canvas; alignment guides assist placement without forced snapping.
- Hold `⇧` to multi-select, drag empty space for marquee selection, `⌘A` to select all, `Esc` to clear selection, and `⌥` + click to cycle through stacked overlapping layers.
- `⌘D` duplicates locally (zero credits); `⌘C` / `⌘V` copies and pastes at cursor position.
- Arrow keys nudge by 1 px (`⇧` + arrow nudges 10 px); `⌘]` / `⌘[` adjusts layer order (with `⇧` for bring-to-front / send-to-back), also accessible from the selection toolbar.
- Use **Layers** on the toolbar to list stacked items and recover occluded images; selecting a generated image also highlights its sibling batch outputs and parent source lineage.
- Use Undo / Redo to step through layout edits, and check the top bar save status / project menu before leaving.
