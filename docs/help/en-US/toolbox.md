# Toolbox Guide

Toolbox handles single or batch product image preparation. Upload photos, adjust settings, and download results individually or as a ZIP package.

## Background Removal

Isolates the product on a transparent background. Includes **20 free images per calendar month** (Asia/Shanghai billing month), with credit pricing displayed before submission once free quota is used. See [Background Removal Guide](/help/bg-remove).

## Watermark

1. Upload the product photos you want to watermark.
2. Configure text or upload a transparent logo PNG, then adjust anchor, scale, and opacity in live preview.
3. Start local rendering and download individual images or a ZIP archive.

## Aspect Ratio

1. Upload images and select a target marketplace preset or custom dimensions.
2. Choose crop focus or padding strategy and verify the live preview (adjust focus manually if needed).
3. Render and download locally, or continue with AI Outpaint when expanding canvas edges (credit cost shown upfront).

## Pipeline

1. Upload product images and configure Aspect Ratio settings.
2. Configure Watermark settings and inspect the combined preview.
3. Run batch processing, review any failed items, and download finished assets.

Leaving Pipeline while a run is active safely pauses remaining items until you return — download completed files before closing the tab if needed.
