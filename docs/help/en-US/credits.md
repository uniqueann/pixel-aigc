# Credits & Billing Guide

## What Are Credits?

Credits are the usage balance in Pixel AIGC. All cloud AI generation and editing jobs deduct credits per action. Your current balance stays visible in the top header — click it anytime to view transaction history or top up.

New accounts automatically receive **30 welcome credits** to explore all features.

## How Pricing Works

- **Image & Video Jobs** (Image Studio, Free Canvas, E-commerce Suite): Priced by selected **model and output resolution / duration**. Every generate button shows the exact credit cost before submission, and Free Canvas also displays an estimated total.
- **Sync Utilities**: Erase and Inpaint cost **5 credits per run**; Outpaint bills by expansion passes (up to **10 credits** maximum shown before running); Background Removal includes **20 free images per month** and costs **1 credit per image** beyond the free quota.

## Reserve & Automatic Settlement

1. Credits are **reserved based on requested output count** at submission time.
2. When the job finishes, **only successful outputs are settled**, and any unused slots are automatically returned.
3. Failed or timed-out jobs **refund credits automatically** — no manual support ticket required.

In short: if no result is produced, you are never charged.

## Purchasing Packs & Refunds

- Click your credit balance in the header to open top-up packs: **Starter Pack** (100 credits), **Standard Pack** (320 credits), and **Studio Pack** (1,150 credits).
- Purchased credits never expire; welcome/bonus credits are not redeemable for cash.
- Cash refunds require manual review, apply only to unused purchased credits, and return via the original payment method.
- Automatic credits returned from failed jobs go directly back to your credit balance without requiring a refund request.

## Checking Credit History

Click **Credits** in the header → **Credit History** to inspect every top-up, bonus, deduction, and refund record. Check here first if you have any billing question.

## Rate Limits

To keep services responsive, usage rate limits apply to certain tools: Erase / Inpaint / Outpaint allow up to 20 requests/hour and 2 concurrent jobs per user; Background Removal / Smart Selection allow up to 60 requests/hour and 3 concurrent jobs per user. When rate-limited, wait briefly as instructed — no credits are deducted.
