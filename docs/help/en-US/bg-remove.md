# Background Removal Guide

Background Removal cleanly isolates your product on a transparent background — the first step in preparing listing-ready product photos.

## Upload Requirements

- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP.
- Up to **20 MB per image**; up to **20 images per batch** and **150 MB total batch size**.
- Recommended resolution under 24 MP on desktop (compress oversized photos first if needed).

## How to Use

1. Open **Toolbox → Background Removal**.
2. Drag and drop or select one or multiple product images.
3. Click **Start processing · Free** (when monthly free quota remains) to run, then download images individually or as a ZIP bundle.

## Pricing

Includes **20 free images every month** (the page displays your remaining monthly free count), then **1 credit per image**. Only successful cutouts are charged; failures never deduct quota or credits.

## Troubleshooting Quality Issues

- **Incomplete subject cutout**: Make sure the product fills a reasonable portion of the frame with even lighting.
- **Rough edges**: Fine hair, lace, or transparent glass can leave minor artifacts. Open **Image Studio → Erase** with a small brush to clean stray fringe, or **Retouch** for edge sharpening.
- **Isolating one object among several**: Use **Smart Selection** inside supported studio tools to click the target item directly.

## FAQ

- **Does Background Removal redraw my product?** No — it segments the real subject pixels 1:1 without regenerating product details.
- **Where are images processed?** Background Removal runs in the cloud; Watermark runs 100% locally in your browser without uploading files.
- **Where can I find completed cutouts?** Visit **My Assets** to browse and re-download recent outputs.
