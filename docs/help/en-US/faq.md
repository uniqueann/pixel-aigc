# FAQ

## Account & Sign-In

- **Which sign-in methods are supported?** Email + password, and one-click Google sign-in.
- **What if I forget my password?** Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the email instructions to reset it.
- **How many credits come with a new account?** Every new account receives **30 free welcome credits**.

## Credits & Billing

- **Am I charged if a generation fails?** No. Credits are reserved upfront, settled only for successful outputs, and automatically refunded on failure or timeout.
- **Do purchased credits expire?** No — purchased credits never expire.
- **Where do I view transactions?** Click your credit balance in the top bar → **Credit History**.
- **Can I request a refund?** Cash refunds are reviewed manually for unused purchased credits only and returned to the original payment method. Bonus credits cannot be cashed out, and failed-task credit returns go straight back to your balance.

## Image Processing

- **Which image formats are supported?** JPG, PNG, and WebP, up to 20 MB per file.
- **What does "Coming Soon" mean?** That capability is still under development and its submit button is temporarily disabled.
- **What should I do if I hit a rate limit?** Wait briefly as shown on screen and retry — no credits are deducted for rate-limited requests.
- **Where do processed images go?** View and download generated images and videos in **My Assets**. Email Assistant history records are retained for **7 days**, so export important drafts promptly.

## Toolbox

- **Is Background Removal free?** You get **20 free images per month**, then 1 credit per image beyond the free quota.
- **What happens if I leave Pipeline mid-run?** Remaining items pause safely; return to the page to resume or download completed images.
- **Does Watermark upload my images?** No — Watermark renders entirely locally in your browser.

## General

- **Can I use Pixel AIGC on mobile?** Yes, layouts adapt to narrow screens, though complex brush masking or canvas work is easiest on desktop.
- **Are my uploaded images used to train AI models?** No. Your uploads are used solely to generate your requested outputs.
- **Found a bug or need help?** See [Contact Support](/help/contact).
