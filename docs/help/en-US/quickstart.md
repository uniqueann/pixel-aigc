# Quick Start

Pixel AIGC is an AI image and copy studio built for e-commerce sellers — covering product cutouts, background replacement, batch generation, and marketing emails in one workspace.

## Core Modules at a Glance

- **[E-commerce Suite](/help/ecommerce-suite)**: Generate standard multi-slot marketplace image sets (pure-white main image, lifestyle scenes, detail close-ups, feature callouts, and dimension overlays) with free layer editing and compliance-checked ZIP export.
- **[Image Studio](/help/workstation)**: 8 dedicated AI photo editing tools — Smart Edit, Relight, Erase, Inpaint, Variations, Fusion, Outpaint, and Retouch.
- **[Toolbox](/help/toolbox)**: High-frequency utilities — Background Removal, Watermark, Aspect Ratio (for marketplace dimensions), plus **Pipeline** chaining Aspect Ratio → Watermark in one batch.
- **[Free Canvas](/help/canvas)**: Create marketing visuals from scratch; Text-to-Image, Text-to-Video, Image-to-Video, and Video Replication are available depending on enabled models.
- **[Email Assistant](/help/email-assistant)**: Choose platform-managed DeepSeek or Gemini models, pay per successful generation, and draft or polish outreach emails individually or in CSV batches.

## Create Your First Image in 5 Minutes

1. Sign up or sign in with Google or email — new accounts automatically receive **30 welcome credits**.
2. Open **Toolbox → Background Removal** and upload a product photo (JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to 20 MB per image).
3. Click **Start processing** to get a transparent cutout in seconds and download it right away.
4. Ready for listing prep? Open **Toolbox → Aspect Ratio**, pick **Amazon Main Image** to resize in one click, then add your brand logo in **Watermark**.

## Recommended Product Listing Workflow

```
Product Photo → Background Removal → Aspect Ratio (Platform Preset) → Watermark → Download / My Assets
```

This chain is built right into **Toolbox → Pipeline**: configure sizing and watermark settings once and batch-process multiple images. Switching tabs pauses processing safely so you can resume when you return.

## How Credits Work

- Every generate button clearly displays the credit cost upfront (e.g. **Generate N · X credits**).
- Credits are reserved on submit, **settled only for successful outputs**, and automatically refunded on failure or timeout.
- Background Removal includes **20 free images per month**, then 1 credit per image thereafter.

See [Credits & Billing Guide](/help/credits) for full pricing and refund rules.
